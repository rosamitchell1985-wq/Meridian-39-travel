Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and how to make us stop.

Effective 08/01/2026  ·  First published 01/15/2024  ·  Meridian Field Guide, Inc.

1. Who we are

Meridian Field Guide, Inc. is an Oregon corporation operating the website at meridianfieldguide.com and the trip planning service available through it. In this policy, "we", "us" and "Meridian" mean that company, and "you" means any visitor to the site or user of the service.

We are the controller of the personal information described here, except where we act as a processor on behalf of a business customer under a white-label agreement, in which case that customer is the controller and their own policy governs.

2. What information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account details. Email address, a display name and a hashed password, if you create an account. We do not require a legal name.
  • Trip content. Destinations, dates, party size, pace, budget tier, interests and any stops you add or remove. This is stored only in your browser unless you create an account.
  • Contact form submissions. Name, email address, optional phone number, topic, subject and message body.
  • Payment details for paid plans. Processed by our payment provider. We receive the last four digits of the card, the card brand, the expiry month and the billing country. We never receive or store the full card number.
  • Custom itinerary intake. Whatever you choose to tell an editor about your trip, which may include accessibility requirements or dietary needs if you disclose them.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. IP address, user agent string, referring URL, requested page and timestamp. Retained for 30 days for security and abuse prevention.
  • Strictly necessary storage. A session identifier for signed-in users, a token protecting forms against cross-site request forgery, and your stored cookie consent choice.
  • Local storage on your device. Saved trips, packing checklist progress and your consent preference. This never leaves your device unless you have an account.
  • Analytics, only with consent. Aggregate page views, approximate region derived from IP address, device category and referrer.
  • Advertising, only with consent. Identifiers set by advertising vendors, described in section 5.

2.3 What we do not collect

We do not collect precise geolocation, biometric data, government identifiers, health information, or information about your race, religion, political opinions, sexual orientation or trade union membership. We do not buy personal information from data brokers. We do not fingerprint devices.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We set strictly necessary cookies without asking, because the service does not function without them. We set nothing else until you make a choice in the consent banner. Rejecting optional cookies does not degrade any feature of the site, including the planner and the free tools.

You can change your choice at any time using the cookie preferences link, which appears in the footer of every page. The complete inventory of cookies, their purposes and their retention periods is in the Cookie Policy.

We honour the Global Privacy Control signal. If your browser sends one, we treat it as an opt-out of sale and sharing under California law and as a rejection of advertising cookies.

7. Security

All traffic is served over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with a modern algorithm and are never stored or transmitted in readable form. Access to production data is restricted to named staff, logged, and reviewed quarterly. Backups are encrypted at rest.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities within the timeframes required by applicable law.

8. Where information is stored

Our servers are located in the United States. If you access the service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored in and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country. Where we transfer personal data out of the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Your California privacy rights (CCPA and CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising them: no price change, no reduced service, no different quality.

  • Right to know. The categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the sources, the business purpose and the categories of third party we disclosed it to.
  • Right to delete. Deletion of personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions such as completing a transaction or complying with tax law.
  • Right to correct. Correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. See section 10 below.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as that term is defined, so there is nothing to limit.
  • Right to portability. A copy of your information in a portable, machine-readable format. Account holders can also export directly from account settings at any time.

How to make a request. Email privacy@meridianfieldguide.com with the subject line "California privacy request", or call +1 (503) 894-2170. We acknowledge within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days, extendable once by a further 45 days where necessary, with notice to you.

Verification. We verify a request by confirming control of the email address on the account, or by matching at least two data points you provide against our records where there is no account. An authorized agent may act for you with written permission, which we will ask to see.

10. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined by California law. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

Because advertising cookies can, under some interpretations, constitute "sharing", we make the opt-out available regardless and treat it as binding. You have three ways to exercise it:

  1. Use the cookie preferences panel. Open cookie preferences and switch advertising off. This takes effect immediately on this device and browser.
  2. Send a Global Privacy Control signal. If your browser or extension sends GPC, we detect and honour it automatically. You do not need to contact us.
  3. Write to us. Email privacy@meridianfieldguide.com with the subject line "Do not sell or share". No account is required and we will not ask you to create one.

We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age.

11. Note for visitors in the EU, EEA and UK

We are based in the United States and our services are directed at travelers in the United States. We do not target advertising or offer goods and services specifically to individuals in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. Nonetheless, if you visit from those regions, the General Data Protection Regulation or the UK GDPR may apply to our processing of your personal data, and we handle it accordingly.

Where the GDPR applies, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. The legal bases we rely on are set out in section 3.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@meridianfieldguide.com. We respond within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority, or with the Information Commissioner's Office in the United Kingdom.

12. Children's privacy

This service is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. Account creation requires an affirmation that you are 13 or older.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we delete it promptly. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided us with personal information should write to privacy@meridianfieldguide.com and we will delete it and confirm within 10 business days.

We do not serve personalized advertising to anyone we know to be under 18, and we do not sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

13. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our practices change. The effective date at the top of the page always reflects the current version. For material changes we post a notice on the site for at least 30 days before the change takes effect, and we email account holders.
