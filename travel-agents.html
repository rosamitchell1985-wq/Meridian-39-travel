The problem this solves

Independent agents lose more hours to document production than to anything else in the business. The research is quick, the supplier relationships are already in place, and the client conversation takes forty minutes. Then somebody spends three hours in a word processor turning it into a document the client will actually read, and does it again from scratch for the next client.

Meridian's white-label plans take the planning engine that powers our public tool and put your branding on the front of it. You build the day plans, adjust the stops, set the pricing that reflects your commissions, and export a typeset PDF with your logo, your colours and your contact details. Nothing on the client-facing document mentions us.

The stop library is yours to extend. Agents who specialize in a region add their own properties, their own operators and their own notes, and those entries stay private to your account. Nothing you write becomes part of our public destination files unless you explicitly offer it, and we pay for anything we publish.

We built this because agents kept writing to ask for it. A meaningful share of the people who first found Meridian as a consumer tool were quietly using it to draft client documents, which was against the letter of our terms and entirely reasonable in spirit. This is the version that is properly licensed for it.

Who this is for

Independent agents
Solo and two-person practices producing between five and forty client itineraries a month.
Small tour operators
Operators running fixed departures who need a consistent document for every guest.
Not for
Large agencies with an existing GDS-integrated mid-office system. We do not integrate with Sabre, Amadeus or Travelport, and we will tell you that on the first call rather than the fifth.

How onboarding works

A genuine four-step sequence, typically completed inside two weeks.

  1. A thirty-minute call

    We look at two of your existing client documents and tell you honestly whether this will save you time. If your itineraries are mostly flight and hotel confirmations rather than day plans, it will not, and we will say so.

  2. Brand setup

    Send us your logo as a PNG or PDF, your two brand colours as hex values, and your contact block. We configure the export templates and send you a sample document within three business days.

  3. Library import

    If you have existing property lists, supplier notes or standard day plans in a spreadsheet, we import them into your private stop library at no charge for the first 500 entries.

  4. Two weeks of parallel running

    Build your next three client itineraries in both your current process and Meridian, and compare the time. If it is not faster, we refund the first quarter in full. That offer is in writing in the agreement, not just on this page.

What this does not do

Said here so that nobody spends a call discovering it.

No GDS integration

Meaning
We do not connect to Sabre, Amadeus or Travelport, and we do not import PNRs. Flight and hotel details are entered manually or pasted in.

No booking or payment processing

Meaning
Meridian produces the plan. Your bookings, your invoicing and your client payments all stay in whatever system you use today.

No commission tracking

Meaning
You can set a markup on any cost line and it flows into the client document. We do not reconcile commissions against supplier statements.

No client CRM

Meaning
Trips are organized by trip, not by client record. If you need a CRM, keep using yours; several of our agencies run this alongside one.

Contract terms

Term
Twelve months, billed annually in advance. Monthly billing available at a 20 percent premium with a 30-day term.
Data ownership
Your client data and your private library are yours. Full export in CSV and PDF at any time, including after cancellation.
Cancellation
Thirty days' notice. See the refund and cancellation policy and the terms of service.
Data processing
We act as a processor for your client data. A data processing addendum is available on request.
