Three things worth checking first

Not because we mind hearing from you, but because these three questions arrive most often and the answers are already written.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Answer
Cancel any time from account settings. Annual plans are refunded pro rata within the first 30 days. Full terms on the refund and cancellation page.

Why can I only save two trips?

Answer
Because two is what the free tier includes, permanently. It is not a trial. The reasoning is on the pricing page.

Can you add my hotel or tour to a destination file?

Answer
Not in exchange for payment, ever. If you believe a place genuinely belongs in a guide, write to the editors with what it is and why, and an editor will read it. That is the whole process and it has no commercial component.
