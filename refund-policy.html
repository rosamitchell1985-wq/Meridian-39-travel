2. How to cancel

Cancel from account settings under Billing. It takes one click and a confirmation, and there is no retention flow, no offer sequence and no telephone requirement. If you cannot access the account, email support@meridianfieldguide.com from the address on the account and we will cancel it for you within one business day.

Cancelling stops future charges. It does not by itself trigger a refund; whether one is due is set out in section 1.

3. What happens to your content

Nothing is deleted when you cancel. Your two most recently edited trips remain fully editable on the free tier, and any additional trips become read-only. You keep full export access to everything, in both cases, indefinitely.

If you close the account entirely, we delete account data within 30 days as described in the Privacy Policy. Export first; we cannot recover it afterwards.

4. Annual plans in detail

Annual Pro is $72.00 charged once. If you cancel within 30 days of the charge, we refund the unused months on a pro rata basis, rounded up to your benefit. For example, cancelling 20 days into an annual term refunds 11 months, or $66.00.

After 30 days, an annual term is not refundable, but cancelling stops the renewal and you keep Pro until the term ends. We email a renewal reminder at least 14 days before each annual charge, so a renewal should never be a surprise. If one is, write to us and we will sort it out; we would rather refund a genuinely unwanted renewal than argue about a date.

5. The custom itinerary service

The service is $149 for a domestic trip and $249 for an international one, charged once at booking.

  • Before the intake call. Full refund, no questions.
  • After the call but before delivery. 50 percent refund, because editorial time has been spent.
  • After delivery. Not refundable. The fee includes two rounds of revisions within fourteen days, and we would much rather revise it until it works.
  • If we miss the five business day deadline. Full refund, automatically, and you keep the itinerary. You do not have to ask.

6. Business plans

Studio and Agency plans run for a twelve-month term billed annually in advance, and cancel on 30 days' written notice with a pro rata refund of the unused term.

The parallel-run guarantee described on the for travel agents page is contractual, not marketing: build three client itineraries in both your current process and in Meridian during the first two weeks, and if it is not faster, we refund the first quarter in full.

7. Refunds we give even when we do not have to

These are discretionary and we apply them consistently rather than case by case.

Duplicate charges

Policy
Refunded in full immediately, without needing to be asked, as soon as we identify one.

An unwanted renewal you did not notice

Policy
Refunded in full if you tell us within 30 days and have not used a Pro feature in that period.

Extended outage

Policy
If the planner is unavailable for more than eight consecutive hours, we credit affected subscribers a full month, automatically.

A feature that does not do what this site says

Policy
Full refund of the current term. If our own description was wrong, that is our error, not your risk.

8. How a refund reaches you

Refunds are returned to the original payment method. We issue them within two business days of approval, and card networks typically post them within a further five to ten business days. We email a confirmation with the amount and the date it was issued.

We cannot refund to a different card or as store credit, because the payment processor will not allow it.

9. Disputes and chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a chargeback. A chargeback costs us a fee and freezes the account while the bank investigates, and in almost every case we would simply have refunded you. Email support@meridianfieldguide.com and we respond within one business day.

If we decline a refund and you disagree, escalate to hello@meridianfieldguide.com, which reaches the founding editor. Your statutory rights under applicable consumer law are not affected by anything in this policy.
