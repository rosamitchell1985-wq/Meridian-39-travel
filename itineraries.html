What a Meridian itinerary is

An itinerary here is a finished document, not a suggestion list. Each day carries a headline, a short editorial note explaining why the day is shaped the way it is, four or five time-stamped stops, the travel time between them, and a total cost for the day covering lodging, food and admissions for the whole party.

Three of the nine are published as full pages with every day written out. The other six are summarized below with the day-by-day skeleton, which is enough to judge whether the shape suits you. All nine destinations are available inside the trip planner, where you can rebuild any of them around your own dates and party size.

How to use these

Reading
Free, no account, no email required.
Adapting
Open the planner, pick the same destination, and cut what does not suit you.
Printing
Every itinerary page is styled for letter and A4 through the browser print dialog.

Six more, in outline

Day-by-day skeletons with the cost and the shape. Build any of them out in the planner in about nine minutes.

Lisbon and Sintra

6 days  ·  $1,704 for two

Six days without a car

Trains and trams do everything. The only day that needs planning is Sintra, which fills by ten.

  • Day 1

    Alfama, the castle, and a fado room

    Walk downhill from Portas do Sol · $268 for two

  • Day 2

    Belem: monastery, tower and the original pastry

    25 min tram from Praca do Comercio · $296 for two

  • Day 3

    Sintra: Pena Palace and Quinta da Regaleira

    40 min train from Rossio · $312 for two

  • Day 4

    LX Factory, MAAT and the Time Out Market

    15 min tram · $284 for two

  • Day 5

    Cascais and the Atlantic coast

    35 min train along the water · $272 for two

  • Day 6

    Azulejo museum, then Chiado for the last afternoon

    20 min bus · $272 for two

Estimated trip cost $1,704

Two travelers, one room, moderate tier. Excludes transatlantic airfare.

Banff and the Icefields Parkway

6 days  ·  $2,364 for two

Six days on shuttle time

Moraine Lake shuttle reservations open in spring and vanish the same week. Everything else is flexible.

  • Day 1

    Calgary to Banff, gondola and the hot springs

    1 hr 40 min drive · $412 for two

  • Day 2

    Moraine Lake sunrise shuttle, then Lake Louise

    55 min shuttle, booked in advance · $398 for two

  • Day 3

    Plain of Six Glaciers teahouse, 8.4 miles

    Trailhead at the lakeshore · $386 for two

  • Day 4

    Icefields Parkway to Peyto and Athabasca

    93 miles on Highway 93 North · $402 for two

  • Day 5

    Johnston Canyon and a Vermilion Lakes canoe

    30 min drive · $394 for two

  • Day 6

    Cave and Basin, then the Bow Valley Parkway out

    Slow drive back to Calgary · $372 for two

Estimated trip cost $2,364

Two travelers, one room, moderate tier, including the rental car and park pass.

New Orleans long weekend

4 days  ·  $1,268 for two

Four days, no car needed

The best long weekend in the country. Streetcars, walking, and one taxi to the swamp.

  • Day 1

    French Quarter walk, beignets, Frenchmen Street

    All on foot · $326 for two

  • Day 2

    WWII Museum, then a Garden District streetcar

    St. Charles line, exact fare · $348 for two

  • Day 3

    Bayou Sauvage kayak, then Preservation Hall

    35 min drive to the launch · $328 for two

  • Day 4

    City Park, the sculpture garden and a po-boy

    25 min streetcar · $266 for two

Estimated trip cost $1,268

Two travelers, one room, moderate tier. Domestic airfare excluded.

Mexico City

5 days  ·  $1,180 for two

Five days at 7,350 feet

Deliberately gentle on day one. Altitude is the only thing that regularly derails this trip.

  • Day 1

    Centro Historico, Bellas Artes, an early night

    15 min metro · $224 for two

  • Day 2

    Anthropology museum and Chapultepec Castle

    20 min metro · $232 for two

  • Day 3

    Teotihuacan pyramids, back by four

    1 hr bus from Terminal Norte · $248 for two

  • Day 4

    Coyoacan, Casa Azul and the market

    35 min metro and taxi · $246 for two

  • Day 5

    Roma Norte taco crawl and Condesa architecture

    Walking · $230 for two

Estimated trip cost $1,180

Two travelers, one room, moderate tier. The best value trip in this index.

New York City

5 days  ·  $2,120 for two

Five days, one borough per day

Structured by geography rather than by attraction, which cuts about ninety minutes of subway time a day.

  • Day 1

    Lower Manhattan, the ferry and the memorial

    Staten Island Ferry, free · $432 for two

  • Day 2

    The High Line, Chelsea Market, a Broadway evening

    15 min subway · $486 for two

  • Day 3

    The Met and Central Park end to end

    20 min subway · $412 for two

  • Day 4

    Brooklyn Bridge at sunrise, then Dumbo

    20 min subway to High Street · $404 for two

  • Day 5

    Flushing food crawl in Queens

    40 min on the 7 train · $386 for two

Estimated trip cost $2,120

Two travelers, one room in Long Island City, moderate tier.

Scottish Highlands and Skye

8 days  ·  $2,592 for two

Eight days on single-track roads

Add ninety minutes a day over whatever the map says. The passing places are the reason.

  • Days 1 to 2

    Edinburgh, then north through the Cairngorms

    Rail then rental car · $648 for two

  • Days 3 to 4

    Glencoe, Glenfinnan and the road to the isles

    124 miles of single track · $652 for two

  • Days 5 to 6

    Skye: the Quiraing, Neist Point and Portree

    Ferry or the Skye Bridge · $664 for two

  • Days 7 to 8

    Applecross pass and the return by Loch Ness

    Bealach na Ba, not in fog · $628 for two

Estimated trip cost $2,592

Two travelers, one room, moderate tier, including the rental car and fuel.
