The planner

How the tool behaves, what it costs and what it will not do.

Yes, and permanently rather than for a trial period. The free tier, which we call Field Notes, keeps two trips indefinitely with the full day-plan builder, time-stamped stops, transit notes, daily costs, all sixteen destination files and all nine sample itineraries. No card is required and no email address is needed to start planning.

Two trips is what the median American traveler actually plans in a year. If that describes you, you should never pay us anything.

Unlimited saved trips, offline PDF export typeset for letter and A4, up to eight collaborators per trip, price tracking on flights and lodging you have flagged, thirty days of version history, ad-free reading, and email support answered within one business day.

Pro costs $9.00 per month or $72.00 per year in US dollars. The full comparison is on the pricing page.

Three lines make up each day. Admissions are the sum of the individual stop prices multiplied by your party size. Meals are a per-person daily figure for your chosen budget tier, covering a counter breakfast, a casual lunch and a sit-down dinner without alcohol. Lodging is a nightly room rate multiplied by the number of rooms, calculated at two travelers per room and rounded up.

Airfare is deliberately excluded, because the same seat can vary by an order of magnitude depending on your departure airport and booking lead time. Use the budget estimator to add it.

Every baseline figure is published in full on the how it works page, and refreshed quarterly.

Every stop was written by a named editor or contributing writer who has been there in person. Not aggregated from reviews, not generated. Each record carries a duration, an admission price, a transit note describing how you arrive from the previous stop, a set of tags, and the name of the writer who filed it with the date of last verification.

We do not accept press trips, comped hotel nights or sponsored excursions, which is why the index has sixteen destinations rather than two hundred.

No. We are a publication, not a travel agency. We link out to operators and booking sites, and where an affiliate relationship exists we say so at the link. Your contract is always with the airline, hotel or operator, never with us.

The site is built mobile first and works down to a 360 pixel viewport. The planner runs entirely in your browser, so once a page has loaded it continues to work without a connection, and your saved plan lives in local storage on that device.

For genuine offline use on a plane, Pro subscribers export a PDF. Everyone else can use the browser print dialog, which we have styled for letter and A4 paper.

Billing, refunds and the business

Money questions, answered without a sales pitch attached.

Five sources, and nothing else. Pro subscriptions, which are roughly two thirds of revenue and fund all editorial payroll. Affiliate commissions from hotel, flight search, car rental and tour partners. The one-time custom itinerary service at $149 domestic and $249 international. Clearly labeled display advertising on editorial pages. And white-label plans for travel agents and small tour operators at $59 to $79 per seat per month.

No partner has ever bought a placement in a destination file, and no editor is compensated on booking volume. The full statement is on the about page and the affiliate disclosure.

No, and the structural safeguard is that no editor sees or is paid on booking data. Affiliate links are added after a guide is written, by a different person, and only to operators the guide already recommends. If a recommendation would change because of who is paying us, our editorial standards say it does not belong in the guide.

Your price is identical whether you use our link or go direct.

Annual Pro plans are refunded pro rata if you cancel within 30 days of the charge. Monthly plans are not refunded for a partial month, but cancelling stops the next charge and you keep access to the end of the period you paid for.

The custom itinerary service is refunded in full if you cancel before the intake call, and at 50 percent if you cancel after the call but before delivery. Full terms are on the refund and cancellation page.

Nothing is deleted. Your two most recently edited trips stay fully editable on the free tier and the rest become read-only. You can export everything, on either plan, at any time, including after cancellation.

There is no automatic discount code, because we would rather set one honest price. If $72 a year is a genuine obstacle, write to support@meridianfieldguide.com and say so. We grant a reduced rate to anyone who asks and we do not ask for documentation.

Data, privacy and accessibility

What is collected, what is not, and how to exercise your rights.

No. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended. The do not sell or share section of the privacy policy explains how to submit a request regardless, and we honour Global Privacy Control signals.

Strictly necessary cookies only, until you choose otherwise. Those cover sign-in, your saved consent choice and basic security. Analytics and advertising cookies, including cookies used by Google, are set only after you accept them in the banner, and you can change your mind at any time through the cookie preferences link in the footer.

The full inventory, with retention periods, is in the cookie policy.

If you have not created an account, entirely in your browser's local storage on that device. Nothing is transmitted to us. Clearing site data removes it and we cannot recover it.

With an account, trips are stored on servers in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest, and are exportable and deletable by you at any time from account settings.

No. This service is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. If we learn that we have, we delete it. A parent or guardian who believes we hold such information should write to privacy@meridianfieldguide.com.

We target WCAG 2.1 Level AA. That means semantic landmarks, a skip-to-content link, visible focus indicators, full keyboard operation, alt text on every image, a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for body text, and full respect for the reduced-motion preference.

Known gaps and how to report a barrier are listed on the accessibility statement. We respond to barrier reports within two business days.

Tell us, please. Write to editors@meridianfieldguide.com with the page, the detail and where you saw the correct information. The named author replies within two business days and any correction is published with a dated note on the page rather than quietly edited in.
