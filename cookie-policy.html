Cookie Policy

Every cookie and storage item this site uses, why it exists, how long it lasts, and how to switch it off.

Effective 08/01/2026  ·  First published 01/15/2024

Meridian Field GuideIssue No. 14 Portland, Oregon · 08/18/2026Page 1

1. The short version

We set strictly necessary cookies without asking, because sign-in, form security and remembering your consent choice do not work without them. We set nothing else until you choose in the banner.

If you reject optional cookies, nothing on this site stops working. The planner, the packing checklists and the budget estimator behave identically. You will see advertising, but it will not be personalized.

You can change your mind at any time using the cookie preferences link in the footer of every page.

2. What a cookie is, and what else we use

A cookie is a small text file a site asks your browser to store and send back on later requests. We also use two related technologies:

  • Local storage. A larger browser store that is never automatically transmitted to a server. This is where your saved trips and packing checklist live if you do not have an account.
  • Session storage. The same idea, cleared when you close the tab. We use it for transient interface state.

Neither is a cookie in the technical sense, but both store data on your device, so we list them here for completeness.

6. How to control cookies

On this site

The banner
Appears on your first visit with three options: accept all, reject optional, or manage preferences with individual switches.
Changing your mind
Open cookie preferences from the footer of any page. The change applies immediately.
Global Privacy Control
If your browser or extension sends a GPC signal, we honour it automatically as a rejection of advertising cookies and as a California opt-out.

In your browser

All major browsers
Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge all let you block or delete cookies in their privacy settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent sign-in from working, on any site.
Clearing site data
This also removes your saved trips and checklist progress, which we cannot recover.

7. Changes and contact

We update this policy whenever a cookie is added or removed. The effective date at the top always reflects the current version, and material changes trigger a fresh consent prompt.
