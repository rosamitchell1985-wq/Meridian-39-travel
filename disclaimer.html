Disclaimer

What the information on this site is, what it is not, and the things you must check yourself before you get on a plane.

Effective 08/01/2026  ·  First published 01/15/2024

1. General information only

Everything published on meridianfieldguide.com, including destination files, sample itineraries, guides, planner output and the free tools, is provided for general informational purposes. It is journalism and software, not professional advice, and it is not tailored to your particular circumstances.

We take care to be accurate. Every stop in our libraries was visited by a named editor or contributor, every destination file carries a last-verified date, and figures are re-checked at least twice a year. Even so, we make no warranty that the content is complete, current or free of error.

2. Information changes without notice

The following change frequently and often without any public announcement. Verify each one directly with the operator or the responsible authority before you rely on it:

  • Prices. Admissions, room rates, fuel, rental cars and fares. All figures on this site are estimates in US dollars, converted at the rate in effect when they were sampled, and are not quotations.
  • Opening hours and closures. Especially for religious sites, seasonal attractions and anything in a national park.
  • Permits and reservation systems. National park timed entry, lotteries and corridor reservations change season to season. Confirm on the park's own nps.gov page for your dates.
  • Entry requirements. Passport validity rules, visas, electronic travel authorizations and vaccination requirements are set by governments and can change quickly. Check the destination country's own immigration authority and the US State Department.
  • Transport. Timetables, road closures, weather restrictions and shuttle operation.

3. We are not a travel agency

Meridian Field Guide, Inc. is a publisher and a software provider. We are not a travel agency, tour operator, airline, hotel, car rental company, insurer or seller of travel, and we do not act as agent for any of them. We take no bookings and hold no client funds.

When you follow a link and transact with a third party, that contract is between you and them, on their terms. We are not a party to it and we are not responsible for their performance, pricing, safety practices or conduct.

4. No professional advice

Nothing on this site is legal, immigration, medical, financial, tax or insurance advice.

  • Health. Our safety sections describe environmental hazards in plain terms. They are not medical guidance. Consult a physician or a travel health clinic about vaccinations, altitude, medications and fitness to travel.
  • Insurance. Where we describe typical coverage levels, that is general orientation, not a recommendation about your policy. Read the policy wording and speak to the insurer.
  • Immigration. Where we describe visa or authorization requirements, treat it as a prompt to check the official source, never as a substitute for it.
  • Money. Cost estimates are planning tools, not budgets or financial projections.

5. Outdoor activity and personal risk

A number of the activities described on this site carry inherent risk, including hiking, glacier walking, canyoneering, wading in rivers and slot canyons, driving on gravel or single-track roads, swimming, and travel at high altitude or in extreme heat or cold.

You are responsible for assessing your own fitness, experience and equipment, and for the decisions you make in the field. Our safety sections are a starting point, not a substitute for a guide, a ranger, a current forecast, or your own judgment on the day. If conditions look wrong, turn around.

6. Third-party content and links

This site links to third-party websites we do not control. We are not responsible for their content, availability, accuracy or privacy practices, and a link is not an endorsement beyond what our own text says.

Some outbound links are affiliate links from which we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. They are labeled at the point of use. See the Affiliate Disclosure.

7. Reviews and reader submissions

Reader reviews published on this site reflect the experience of the individual who wrote them at the time they travelled. They are not a prediction of your experience, and we do not verify the underlying facts of a personal account beyond confirming that the reader used the service.

8. Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Meridian Field Guide, Inc., its officers, employees and contributors accept no liability for any loss, injury, delay, expense or damage arising from reliance on information published on this site or produced by the planner. Your use of this site is at your own risk. The full limitation is set out in section 11 of the Terms of Service.

9. Corrections

If something here is wrong, please tell us. Write to editors@meridianfieldguide.com with the page, the detail and where you found the correct information. The named author replies within two business days, and corrections are published with a dated note on the page rather than edited in quietly.
