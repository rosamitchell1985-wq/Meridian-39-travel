Three rules that matter more than the list

Pack for four days
Not for ten. Detergent sheets weigh nothing and a hotel sink takes ten minutes. This single change halves most people's luggage.
Split across bags
If two of you are checking bags, put half of each person's clothes in the other bag. One lost bag then costs you nothing.
Walk in the shoes first
Twelve miles, at home, before the trip. More holidays are ruined by new footwear than by weather.

Why twelve percent

What it covers
Checked bag fees, tips, parking, laundry, one taxi you did not plan, one dinner that was worth it, and the souvenir nobody admits to buying.
Where the number came from
Comparing planned budgets against actual spend reported by readers after their trips. Twelve percent is the median gap, not a round number we liked.
When to raise it
Use twenty percent for a first international trip, and for any trip involving children.

What these tools do with your data

Nothing you type on this page is transmitted to us or to anyone else. The checklist state is stored in your browser's local storage on this device, under a single key, and is removed when you clear site data or press the clear button above.

The budget estimator holds nothing at all. The calculation happens in the page and the numbers disappear when you close the tab.

Neither tool requires an account, an email address, or consent to any optional cookie. If you have rejected optional cookies, both tools work exactly as they do for everyone else. Full detail is in the privacy policy and the cookie policy.
