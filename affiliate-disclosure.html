Affiliate Disclosure

Some links on this site earn us a commission. Here is exactly which ones, how much, and what stops that from changing what we write.

Effective 08/01/2026  ·  First published 01/15/2024

Meridian Field GuideIssue No. 14 Portland, Oregon · 08/18/2026Page 1

1. The disclosure, stated plainly

Meridian Field Guide, Inc. participates in affiliate advertising programs. This means that when you click certain links on this site and then make a booking or purchase, we may receive a commission from the seller. This costs you nothing extra; the price is identical whether you use our link or go to the seller directly.

This disclosure is made in accordance with the Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising, 16 CFR Part 255.

2. Where you will see it

We do not rely on this page alone. An affiliate relationship is disclosed at three levels:

  • At the link. Every affiliate link carries a visible label next to it reading "affiliate link" in the same size and colour as the surrounding text. Not in a tooltip, not in grey six-point type at the bottom of the page.
  • In the section. Any section of a destination file or itinerary containing an affiliate link opens with an italic note saying so, before the recommendation.
  • In the footer. A standing disclosure appears in the footer of every page on this site.

If you ever find an affiliate link on this site without a label, that is a mistake. Tell us at editors@meridianfieldguide.com and we will fix it the same day.

4. How editorial independence is protected

A disclosure alone is not much of a safeguard. These are the structural rules that sit behind it, and they are in our editorial standards document, not just on this page.

Editors are not paid on bookings

Rule
No editor, writer or contributor receives any compensation tied to booking volume, click volume or affiliate revenue. Contributors are paid a flat day rate plus expenses.

Links are added after the writing

Rule
A guide is written and edited first. Affiliate links are then added by a different person, and only to operators the guide already recommends. No link is ever the reason a recommendation exists.

Editors do not see booking data

Rule
Affiliate reporting is visible to two people in the business, neither of whom commissions or edits content.

No placements are for sale

Rule
No hotel, operator or destination board can buy a mention, a ranking or an inclusion in a destination file, an itinerary or a guide. We have declined these offers, and if a partner made acceptance a condition of the relationship we would end the relationship and write about it.

No press trips

Rule
We do not accept hosted travel, comped hotel nights, free excursions, upgrades or gifts, in whole or in part. We pay for every trip we report on. This is why the destination index has sixteen entries rather than two hundred.

A negative review is still published

Rule
If a place we have an affiliate relationship with is not worth your money, we say so, and the link comes out. This has happened.

5. What data an affiliate link shares

When you click a labeled affiliate link, the destination site and the affiliate network receive a referral identifier telling them the visit originated with us, and they may set their own cookie on their own domain. That is how a commission is attributed.

We do not send them your name, your email address, your trip content or anything you typed into the planner. Once you are on their site, their privacy policy governs, not ours. Our own handling is described in the Privacy Policy, and the cookies involved are listed in the Cookie Policy.

6. How to avoid affiliate links entirely

If you would rather we did not earn anything from your booking, that is completely fine and we mean that. Two ways:

  • Read the recommendation, then open a new tab and go to the operator directly by typing their name. The guide is identical either way and none of it is paywalled.
  • Reject advertising cookies in the cookie preferences panel. This does not remove affiliate links, which are plain hyperlinks, but it does stop advertising vendors from setting identifiers.

Nothing on this site is gated behind an affiliate click, and no content changes based on whether you use one.

7. Advertising is separate

Display advertising on this site is separate from affiliate income. Ad slots are clearly labeled "Advertisement", they sit below the main content of a page, they never appear on legal pages or the 404 page, and advertisers have no influence over editorial content and no advance sight of it.

8. Questions

If anything here is unclear, or if you believe a link is unlabelled or a recommendation looks bought, write to us. We will answer specifically rather than with a form response.
