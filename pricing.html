Pricing and Plans

Free for two trips.
Nine dollars if you plan more.

No trial countdown, no card required to start, and nothing that quietly stops working after a fortnight.

The high ground, which is where most of our subscribers are going. Photograph via Unsplash.

Two plans for individuals

All prices in US dollars. Annual billing works out to $6.00 a month, a saving of $36 a year against paying monthly.

Field Notes

$0Free, permanently

For the traveler who takes one real trip and one long weekend a year, which is most people.

  • Two saved trips, kept indefinitely
  • The full day-plan builder with time-stamped stops
  • Travel time between stops and estimated daily cost
  • All sixteen destination files and nine sample itineraries
  • All free tools: packing checklists and the budget estimator
  • One collaborator per trip
  • Print from the browser, styled for letter and A4

Start planning, no card

Pro

$9per month, or $72 billed annually

For people who plan constantly, manage trips for a family, or want the itinerary readable on a plane with the wifi off.

  • Everything in Field Notes
  • Unlimited saved trips
  • Offline PDF itinerary export, typeset for letter and A4
  • Up to eight collaborators per trip, each able to edit
  • Price tracking on flights and lodging you have flagged
  • Ad-free reading across the whole publication
  • Version history, so you can undo a day you regret cutting
  • Email support answered within one business day

One-time services and add-ons

Priced individually rather than bundled, so you can see exactly what each thing costs.

Custom itinerary, domestic

$149One time, per trip

A named editor reviews your draft, rebuilds the day plans and returns a finished document within five business days.

  • A 30-minute intake call or a written brief, your choice
  • Full rebuild of every day, not a review of yours
  • Lodging neighbourhood recommendations with reasoning
  • Two rounds of revisions inside fourteen days
  • Delivered as a typeset PDF and inside your account

Request a custom itinerary

Custom itinerary, international

$249One time, per trip

The same service for trips outside the United States, including multi-city routing and entry requirement checks.

  • Everything in the domestic service
  • Entry document and authorization checks for your passport
  • Intercity rail or flight routing between bases
  • Accessibility and dietary requirement handling
  • Delivered within five business days

How we make money, in plain English

Five sources. There is nothing else, and none of them changes what a destination file says.

Pro subscriptions

Share of revenue
Roughly two thirds, and all of the editorial payroll.
Why it matters
Subscriber money is the reason the destination files do not read like advertising.

Affiliate commissions

From whom
Hotel, flight search, car rental and tour partners.
What it costs you
Nothing. The price is the same whether you use our link or not.
How it is handled
Labeled at the link, not only in the footer. No editor is compensated on booking volume. Read the full disclosure.

Custom itinerary service

Price
$149 domestic, $249 international, one time.
Who buys it
Mostly people planning a once-a-decade trip with dates they cannot move.

Advertising and B2B

Display ads
